Oil Field Services

With an experienced team and the right equipment, Crudo offers the full range of upstream oil and gas field services — from exploration through to production.

Field support across the well lifecycle

Upstream work is unforgiving of gaps between vendors. Crudo covers the field scope as a single service line, so the crew that maintains the rig, the unit that pulls the rods and the tools they use all come from one accountable team working to one schedule.

What we deliver

Rig operations & maintenance

Operations and maintenance support for drilling and workover rigs, keeping equipment available through the programme.

Pulling units & rod running

Pulling unit services and rod running crews for routine and remedial well work.

Artificial lift systems

Design and installation of artificial lift systems matched to well conditions and production targets.

Well intervention

Intervention services to restore, maintain or improve production from existing wells.

Rig handling tools

Supply and support of rig handling tools for safe, efficient tubular and equipment handling on the floor.

Sucker rod handling tools

Sucker rod handling tools, rod BOPs and the associated accessories for rod-pumped wells.

Field scopes are frequently combined with our tubular inspection and manpower divisions — for example, an inspection campaign resourced with our own crews during a workover window. Ask about a combined scope.

How the work runs

  • Scope agreed against your programme, well conditions and acceptance criteria
  • Crew, tools and consumables mobilised to the site window
  • Work executed to specification with findings recorded as they occur
  • Reporting and documentation handed over on completion
  • Follow-on support for the remedial work the results point to
