Tubular Inspection Services

Comprehensive inspection solutions — from fundamental tubular checks through highly critical evaluation — built on quality and reliability.

Inspection you can put your name to

At Crudo, we deliver comprehensive inspection solutions ranging from fundamental tubular inspections to highly critical evaluation services. Our specialised expertise is built on a foundation of quality and reliability: every service is carried out in strict compliance with the latest API specifications and DS-1 Category 1–4 requirements, while also aligning fully with client-specific standards.

Standards applied

  • Latest API specifications
  • DS-1 Category 1
  • DS-1 Category 2
  • DS-1 Category 3
  • DS-1 Category 4
  • Client acceptance criteria

Client criteria are applied alongside the published standard, not as a substitute for it. Where your requirements are tighter than the specification, the tighter figure governs.

What we inspect

Casing — all sizes

Comprehensive inspection and support services for casing across all size ranges.

Tubing — all sizes

Reliable inspection and maintenance for tubing of every specification and dimension.

Drill pipe — all sizes

Complete inspection and evaluation of new and used drill pipe, regardless of size.

Bottom hole assembly (BHA)

Specialised inspection for BHA components, including:

  • Drill collars
  • Heavy weight drill pipe (HWDP)
  • Stabilisers
  • Cross-overs
  • Other critical downhole tools

Special tools

Inspection and maintenance services tailored to a wide range of specialised drilling tools.

Reporting & documentation

Findings recorded per joint and reported in a form that stands up to audit and hand-back.
