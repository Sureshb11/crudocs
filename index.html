About Crudo

Excellence in oilfield services, inspection and supply

Crudo is a technical and consulting services company serving the upstream oil and gas sector and the industries around it. Our work rests on three things: technical expertise, well-maintained equipment and an experienced workforce that knows what a wellsite actually demands.

From exploration through production, we support clients with end-to-end oilfield solutions. Our inspection work is carried out to API and DS-1 standards, and our chemical supply division serves oil and gas, petrochemical and construction customers with dependable, documented product.

  • Compliance-first inspection practice
  • Experienced, certified field crews
  • Client-specific acceptance criteria
  • Onshore and offshore deployment

Why Crudo

Built for work where failure is expensive

In this industry, a missed defect or an unqualified hand costs far more than the service that should have caught it. Everything below exists to keep that from happening.

Standards, not approximations

Inspection to the latest API specifications and DS-1 Category 1–4 requirements, with client-specific criteria applied alongside — not instead of — the published standard.

People who have done it before

Pre-screened engineers, technicians and tradesmen with the certifications the job requires, mobilised for onshore or offshore work at the scale the project needs.

Documentation you can hand over

Inspection findings and product certification recorded and reported so the paperwork stands up to audit, hand-back and the next operator down the line.

Scheduled around your rig time

Crews, tools and consumables planned against your programme, because inspection and workover windows do not move to suit the vendor.

Supply that arrives complete

Chemicals and handling tools delivered in the grades and quantities ordered, with the specification documents attached to the consignment.

Safety carried into the field

How we work

A straightforward route from enquiry to hand-back
