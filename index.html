How Crudo works

We are not a catalogue. We are a buyer's agent.

Most exporters sell you what they happen to stock. We work the other way round: you tell us the product, the grade and the quantity, and we go and find the vendor who can actually supply it.

You name the product

Anything from a single spice grade to a mixed food container. If it can be bought in India and legally exported, it is worth asking us about.

We vet the vendor

We identify suppliers, compare grades and pricing, and check the goods against your specification before anything is committed.

We handle the export

Packing, inspection, documentation and freight booking — one invoice, one point of contact, goods on the water.

Why buyers use an agent

Buying from a distance is where deals go wrong

A wrong grade, a short-shipped container or a missing certificate costs far more than the margin on the order. Everything below exists to stop that happening.

Someone on the ground

We are in India, at the vendor's gate. You are not buying from a website and hoping.

Paperwork that clears

Invoice, packing list, origin, phytosanitary, health and fumigation certificates prepared for your customs.

Grades stated plainly

Moisture, purity, size, packing — agreed in writing before the order, checked before it ships.

Honest lead times

Seasonal crops have seasons. We tell you when something is available rather than promising it now.

One invoice, many vendors

Mixed loads from several suppliers consolidated into a single shipment and a single settlement.

Delivered to your terms

Quoted FOB or CIF, shipped by sea or air, to the port and schedule that suits your side.
