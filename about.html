Our story

Excellence across the upstream sector and beyond

At Crudo, we are committed to delivering excellence across the upstream oil and gas sector and the industries connected to it. With a foundation of technical expertise, advanced equipment and an experienced workforce, we provide end-to-end solutions built for reliability, safety and quality at every stage.

Our core strengths lie in four areas: oil field services, tubular inspection, chemical supply and technical manpower. From exploration to production, we support clients with comprehensive oilfield solutions. Our inspection expertise is governed by API and DS-1 standards, while our chemical division serves oil and gas, petrochemical and construction customers with dependable product.

Driven by integrity and customer focus, Crudo works as a partner to industries where performance and reliability are non-negotiable — delivering services that meet global standards while staying aligned to each client's specific requirements.

Our mission

To give operators a service partner they do not have to check up on — by holding every job to the published standard and the client's own criteria, resourcing it with people who are genuinely qualified, and reporting what we found plainly and on time.

Our vision

To be the technical services company that oil and gas operators across the region turn to first — recognised less for how much we promise than for how consistently the work holds up in the field.

What we stand for

Four commitments that shape how we work

These are not aspirations on a wall. They are the criteria we hold jobs to.

Integrity

We report what the inspection actually found. An inconvenient result is still the result, and hiding it only moves the cost downstream.

Quality

Compliance with API and DS-1 is the floor, not the target. Client-specific criteria are applied on top of the standard, never in place of it.

Safety

Personnel are recruited and deployed against international safety expectations, so the crew that reaches your site already works your way.

Customer focus

Scopes get shaped around the programme in front of us. Standard packages are a starting point, not the answer to every job.

Quality, health & safety

Compliance is the product

In inspection and manpower supply, the deliverable is confidence. That only holds if the standard is applied the same way on every job, and if the people doing the work are certified to do it.

  • Inspection carried out to the latest API specifications
  • DS-1 Category 1 through Category 4 evaluation
  • Client-specific acceptance criteria applied alongside the standard
  • Personnel screened against industry certification requirements
  • Findings recorded and reported for audit and hand-back
  • Chemical consignments supplied with specification documentation
