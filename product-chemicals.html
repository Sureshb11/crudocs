Industrial chemicals

A wide range of chemicals for industry — with a primary focus on oil and gas, petrochemical and construction applications.

Right grade, right quantity, right paperwork

Crudo offers a wide range of chemicals to various industries — mostly oil and gas, petrochemical and construction. The value in chemical supply is rarely the molecule; it is whether the grade matches the specification, the quantity arrives complete, and the documentation comes with it.

Core product range

KCl — Potassium chloride

Widely used in drilling and completion brines for shale inhibition and clay stabilisation.

CaCl2 — Calcium chloride

Used in higher-density brine systems, completion fluids and construction applications.

NaCl — Sodium chloride

The workhorse brine salt, supplied in the grades required for fluid make-up.

Corrosion inhibitors

Inhibitor products to protect tubulars, vessels and pipework in service.

The list above is our core range, not the limit of it. If you need a grade or product that is not shown, ask — sourcing to specification is exactly what we do. Send your requirement.

Industries served

  • Oil & gas — drilling, completion and production
  • Petrochemical processing
  • Construction
  • Allied industrial users

How we supply

  • Requirement confirmed against grade, quantity and delivery point
  • Specification documentation supplied with the consignment
  • Packaging suited to the site and the handling available
  • Repeat and scheduled supply arrangements for ongoing operations
  • Export shipments documented and freighted the same way as our food orders

Handling and transport

Chemical consignments carry classification, packing and transport requirements that vary by product and destination. These are confirmed per order before despatch, and the relevant safety documentation travels with the goods.
