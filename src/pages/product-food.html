Food & agri products

Whatever food product you need out of India, we arrange it — sourced from the growers, mills and farms that actually supply it, and shipped to your port.

There is no fixed catalogue

The lists on this page are the products buyers ask us for most often. They are a starting point, not a boundary. Food sourcing in India is a question of finding the right vendor for the grade you want, at the season you want it — and that is the work we do.

Send us the product, the grade or specification, the quantity and the destination port. If it can be bought in India and legally exported, we will come back with a vendor, a price and a lead time. If we cannot supply it well, we will say so rather than take the order.

Not on the list? That is normal. Most enquiries we receive name something we have to go and source. Send the requirement and we will tell you honestly whether we can do it well.

How we agree a grade

Most disputes in food export come from a specification that was never written down. Before an order is placed we confirm, in writing, the things that decide whether the goods are acceptable:

  • Moisture content
  • Purity and admixture limits
  • Size, grade or count
  • Colour and appearance
  • Packing type and net weight
  • Shelf life at despatch

Seasonality is real

Agricultural goods do not exist all year at the same price or quality. Where a product is out of season we will say so and give you the realistic window, rather than quote you something that will arrive as a disappointment. That honesty is the point of using an agent in the first place.
