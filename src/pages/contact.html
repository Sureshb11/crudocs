Common questions

Before you write to us

Which standards does Crudo inspect to?
All tubular inspection work is carried out in strict compliance with the latest API specifications and DS-1 Category 1 to 4 requirements. Client-specific acceptance criteria are applied alongside the published standard — where your criteria are tighter, the tighter figure governs.
Does Crudo work offshore as well as onshore?
Yes. Our technical manpower and field services divisions support both onshore facilities and offshore installations.
Can services be combined into a single scope?
Yes, and most of our work is structured that way. Oil field services, tubular inspection, chemical supply and technical manpower can be packaged into one scope with a single point of responsibility and one schedule.
What information should I include in an enquiry?
For inspection: the string details, size range and applicable standard. For field services: the programme and the window. For chemicals: product, grade, quantity and delivery point. For manpower: roles, certifications, headcount and mobilisation date.
Which industries does Crudo serve?
Primarily oil and gas, across upstream, midstream and downstream operations. The chemical supply division also serves petrochemical and construction customers.
