Working here

Trading is a detail business. The people who do well at Crudo are the ones who check the number rather than assume it, who would rather deliver bad news early than explain a rejected container later, and who can hold a straight conversation with a farmer in Tamil and a buyer in Dubai on the same afternoon.

Functions we recruit into

Sourcing & procurement

Identifying and assessing vendors, negotiating on grade and price, and holding suppliers to the specification.

Quality control

Sampling, pre-shipment inspection, coordinating laboratory testing and reporting findings honestly.

Export documentation

Preparing invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin and the destination-specific paperwork that clears customs.

Logistics & shipping

Freight booking, container planning, reefer coordination and keeping buyers informed of where their cargo is.

Commercial & business development

Building buyer relationships abroad and turning enquiries into repeat business.

Administration & accounts

Order administration, compliance records and settlement with vendors and buyers.

How to apply

There is no application portal — email is faster and reaches a person. Send your CV to info@crudocs.com and include:

  • The function or trade you are applying for
  • Your certifications, and their expiry dates where relevant
  • Years of experience and the sectors you have worked in
  • Your current location and whether you can relocate or travel
  • Notice period and availability

We read what comes in. If your profile fits something current you will hear from us; otherwise your CV stays on file for future requirements.
